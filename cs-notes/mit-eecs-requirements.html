6-1 Electrical Science & Engineering
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 1 programming skills subject (6.100A or 6.100L)
  • 1 math subject (18.03 or 18.032)
  • 3 foundation subjects (6.1910, 6.2000, 6.3000)
  • 3 header subjects (selectable from 6.2210, 6.2300, 6.2500, 6.3010, 6.3900, 6.4810, 6.6210, 6.C01&6.S052)
  • 6 elective subjects (2 from AUS2 advanced list + 4 from EECS list)
  • Constraints: ≥1 DLAB2, ≥1 independent inquiry, ≥1 probability, ≥2 CI-M
6-2 Electrical Engineering & CS
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 4 fundamental subjects (6.100A or 6.100L, 6.120A or 6.1200, 6.1210, 6.1900)
  • 2 math subjects (1 linear algebra from 6.S084/18.C06/18.06; 1 probability from 6.3700/6.3800/18.05)
  • 4 system design subjects (6.1910, 6.2000, 6.3100, 6.9000)
  • 6 elective subjects (2 from an EE Track + 2 from a different EE Track + 2 from EECS list)
  • Constraints: ≥2 CI-M, ≥1 project-based design lab
6-3 Computer Science & Engineering
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 2 programming skills subjects (6.100A or 6.100L, and 6.1900)
  • 1 discrete math (6.1200)
  • 1 6-3 math subject (selectable from 18.05, 18.06, 18.C06, 6.3700, 6.3800)
  • 3 foundation subjects (6.1010, 6.1210, 6.1910)
  • 3 header subjects (6.1020; 6.1400 or 6.1220; 6.1800)
  • 6 elective subjects (2 from a CS Track + 2 from a different AI+D/CS/EE Track + 2 from EECS list)
  • Constraints: ≥2 AUS2, ≥2 CI-M, ≥1 independent inquiry
6-4 AI & Decision Making
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 1 programming skills subject (6.100A or 6.100L)
  • 3 math subjects (6.1200; 1 linear algebra from 6.S084/18.C06/18.06; 1 probability from 6.3700/6.3800/18.05)
  • 2 foundation subjects (6.1010, 6.1210)
  • 5 Center subjects (1 each from Data-centric, Model-centric, Decision-centric, Computation-centric, Human-centric lists)
  • 2 communication-intensive subjects (1 Application CI-M + 1 CI-M)
  • 2 elective subjects (1 from AI+D AUS or Application CI-M + 1 from EECS or Math)
  • Constraints: ≥1 SERC
6-7 CS & Molecular Biology
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 2 introductory subjects (6.100A&6.100B or 6.1020; and 6.1010)
  • 1 discrete math (6.1200)
  • 2 CS foundation subjects (6.1210, 6.1220)
  • 2 chemistry subjects (5.12; and 5.601&5.602 or 20.110)
  • 1 introductory lab (from 20.109, 6.4880, or 7.002&7.003)
  • 3 Bio foundation subjects (7.03, 7.05, 7.06)
  • 2 elective subjects (1 computational biology + 1 biology restricted)
  • 1 CI-M subject (6.UAR, 6.UAT, or 7.19)
6-14 CS, Economics & Data Science
  • GIR (General Institute Requirements)
  • 3 math subjects (1 linear algebra from 6.S084/18.06/18.C06; 1 probability from 6.3700/14.30/18.600; and 6.1200)
  • 5 computation & algorithms subjects (6.100A; 6.1010 or 6.100B; 6.1210; 6.1220; 6.3900)
  • 3 economics subjects (1 from 14.01/14.03; 14.32; 1 from 14.05/14.18/14.33)
  • 1 optimization/networks subject (from 15.053, 6.3260, 6.7201)
  • 3 elective subjects (1 ECONDS + 1 ECONTH + 1 additional)
  • 1 CI-M subject (15.276, 6.UAR, or 6.UAT)
