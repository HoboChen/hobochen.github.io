I’m Jingqi — a software engineer at Microsoft since 2019, working out of the Redmond area.

I studied Computer Science at Xi’an Jiaotong University and used to compete in OI and ICPC. Before Microsoft, I interned or worked at Alibaba Cloud, YITU Tech, and Hulu. My resume is here.

This is my personal notebook — a mix of Computer Science / Math notes, logs of something, and occasional reflections on life. Unless clearly stated otherwise, all contents may be reused under a CC-BY-NC license.

The name Interpositive is borrowed from analog filmmaking — an interpositive is the intermediate print struck from an original camera negative, sitting between the raw capture and the final release. This site works in much the same way: a middle layer between the raw events, thoughts, and sensations of my life and whatever impression of me forms in someone else’s memory.
